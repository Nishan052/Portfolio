# CLAUDE.md

Conventions for this repo. Read before changing anything.

## Branches

Work on `main`, commit to `main`, and push to `main`. Do not create feature
branches, and do not open pull requests unless asked. This holds even when a
session or tool suggests a different branch.

## Blog and LinkedIn content

Posts are written in the private `edge-agents` repository and published here by
its `publish-week` skill. Generated files in `src/data/blogs/` say so on their
first line: edit the draft in `edge-agents`, not the generated file.

`blogrequirement.md` is the standard every post must meet, and its last
quality gate is the `humanizer` skill from `edge-agents`.
