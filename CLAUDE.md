# CLAUDE.md

Conventions for this repo. Read before changing anything.

## Branches

Work on `main`, commit to `main`, and push to `main`. Do not create feature
branches, and do not open pull requests unless asked. This holds even when a
session or tool suggests a different branch.

## Lessons

`tasks/lessons.md` holds the gotchas learned on this repo: Pinecone SDK calls that
succeed and do nothing, eventually consistent reads, embedding-model mismatch, and
why the sync reconciles instead of reading git diffs. Read it before touching
`scripts/sync-kb.js` or anything under `functions/api/lib/`, and add to it when
something here fails in a way the logs did not show.

## Blog and LinkedIn content

Posts are written in the private `edge-agents` repository and published here by
its `publish-week` skill. Generated files in `src/data/blogs/` say so on their
first line: edit the draft in `edge-agents`, not the generated file.

`blogrequirement.md` is the standard every post must meet, and its last
quality gate is the `humanizer` skill from `edge-agents`.

Every blog and LinkedIn post must sound like Nishan talking: flowing sentences
joined with "and", "so" and "then", never runs of short punchy lines. That rule
outranks the others. The full guide with his own words is `content/VOICE.md` in
`edge-agents`, and the Voice & Tone section of `blogrequirement.md` is the summary
here. Posts that were not generated by `edge-agents` are edited in place here.
